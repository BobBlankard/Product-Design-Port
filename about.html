About

Hey, I’m Owen. I design and build digital products.

I design products people understand quickly and teams can ship confidently.

I recently graduated from SDSU and focus on product design with strong UI craft and implementation awareness. I like taking ambiguous problems and turning them into clear product flows that are ready to build.

My process is practical: define the user problem, design interaction paths that reduce friction, and iterate with engineering until the product feels reliable.

I bring a mix of systems thinking and visual precision. I care about clear hierarchy, usability first, and polished details that improve trust without adding noise.

If you are hiring for product design or UI/UX, this portfolio shows how I work from idea to shipped outcome.

What I bring

Product usability first. Design decisions grounded in implementation reality. Minimal, intentional UI that helps users move from understanding to action.

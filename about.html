About

Hey, I’m Owen. I design and build digital products.

I like making things that feel simple, useful, and human.

I recently graduated from SDSU, and most of my work sits between product design, software engineering, and visual art. I enjoy taking ideas from rough concepts all the way to shipped products.

I care a lot about flow - not just how a screen looks, but how the full experience works over time. I focus on clear structure, thoughtful interactions, and systems that can scale without getting messy.

Outside of product work, I make art and built a 10k+ organic audience through xtc.ngl. That creative side influences my design style: strong typography, intentional spacing, and attention to detail.

I am happiest working on real products with real users, especially when design and engineering need to move together.

Core Focus

Product usability first

I optimize for products people understand quickly and keep using.

Build Stack

Design decisions backed by implementation reality.

Style

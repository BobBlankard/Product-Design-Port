- Spacing system must be consistent and generous; no cramped layouts.
- Motion: scroll-rich (multiple animated properties, stagger), first-view text slide + blur resolving + fade; still premium, not cheap flashy.

- Color: white, black, greyish off-white, blur layers — no orange; avoid saturated accents by default. Black-on-black only for large readable surfaces with matte vs gloss separation when it truly helps.
